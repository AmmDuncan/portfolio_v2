# Desk journey: future ideas

Ideas for `/desk` to pick up once the current build ships. Not scheduled.

## Underwater scenery change (Ammiel, 2026-11-18)

A playful scene switch: the room fills with water and the desk goes underwater.

- The scenery transitions smoothly from the night room to an underwater look: light shafts, caustics on the desk and wall, drifting particles, a colour shift to deep blue/teal.
- Buoyancy: the objects on the desk (controller, phone, books, maybe the laptop) lift off and float, drifting and bobbing so the layout gets displaced.
- It should reverse cleanly back to the normal desk.

Open questions:
- Trigger: a hidden toggle, an easter egg (e.g. click the light switch), or a stop at the end of the scroll?
- Do the screens keep working underwater (Work list scrollable), or is it purely decorative?
- Reduced motion: a static underwater still or a plain cross-fade, no floating.
